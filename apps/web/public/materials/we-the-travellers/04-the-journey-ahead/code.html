Concept 4: Estimation & Rounding

Concept 4: Estimation

Imagine you're driving to the grand canyon. Your car breaks down at km 2250. Do you walk back to km 2000 or push forward to 3000?

rule The "Bossy Neighbour" Method

  • 1 Underline the digit you are rounding to. (e.g., hundreds)
  • 2 Look right at the "bossy neighbour".
  • 3 If it's 0-4, let the underlined digit rest.
  • 4 If it's 5-9, raise the underlined digit by one!
