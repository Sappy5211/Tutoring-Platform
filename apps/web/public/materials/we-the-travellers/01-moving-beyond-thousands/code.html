Moving Beyond Thousands

Sometimes, 4 digits just aren't enough! Think about measuring the distance across an ocean or counting the people in a big city. We need more space for bigger numbers!

visibility

How do we read it?

  • 1

    Start from the right side (the Ones).

  • 2

    Count three spots to the left: 1, 2, 3...

  • 3

    Put a comma! This comma says "Thousand".

45,231

"Forty-five thousand, two hundred thirty-one"

lightbulb Worked Example

Write the number 82,045 in words.

Notice where the comma is. The digits before the comma are "82". We read those, then say "Thousand".

Answer:

Eighty-two thousand, forty-five.

warning

Watch Out!

When you see a zero, like in 30,050, we don't say the zero out loud. We just skip over it when reading! It's "Thirty thousand, fifty", NOT "Thirty thousand, zero hundred, fifty".
