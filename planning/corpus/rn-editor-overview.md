# Outlining

Your notes are made up of bullet points. Each bullet represents a unique piece of knowledge and can be used to generate a flashcard for spaced repetition practice. The hierarchical bullet-point editor (with unlimited nesting) makes it easy to organize knowledge.

- Press Enter to create a new bullet.

- Press Tab to indent a bullet under the bullet above it.

- Press Shift+Tab to outdent a bullet (move it up one level).


Once you have a hierarchy of bullet points, you can click on any one of them or press Ctrl+' (Cmd+' on a Mac) to zoom in, i.e., show only that bullet and its descendants. Go back using your browser's back button or the breadcrumbs at the top of the document, or press Ctrl+; to zoom out one level (Cmd+; on a Mac).

You can move any bullet to a new location by dragging and dropping its bullet point. Move the mouse left and right to control what indentation level it ends up at. (Learn more about [moving and organizing hierarchies](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/6030548-moving-rems-organizing-hierarchies).)

# Link to other bullets

- Add a **[Reference](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/6030714-rem-references)** (link) to another bullet in your Knowledge Base by typing `[[` or `@`.\
\
- Link to a **date** by typing `!!`. See [Daily Documents](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/6752031-daily-documents) for details.\
\
- Add a **[tag](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/6030770-tags)** by typing `##`. They show up on the right.\
\
\
# Add media and special content types\
\
- Add **[images](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/6752220-adding-images-and-media)** by pasting from your clipboard or dragging and dropping, or type `/image` and paste a URL.\
\
- Add **[videos](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/6752220-adding-images-and-media)** by typing `/video`.\
\
- Add **links to websites** by pasting the URL.\
\
- Add **[mathematical formulas](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/6565191-writing-equations-with-latex)** using LaTeX by typing `$$`.\
\
- Make **[todo lists](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/6752161-managing-tasks-with-todos)** by typing `/todo` or pressing Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Return on a Mac).\
\
\
# Format text\
\
- Add **[inline formatting](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/6030579-formatting-your-notes)** (such as bold or italics) to specific text by highlighting it and selecting the appropriate option from the editor toolbar.\
\
\
\

\
- Add a **highlight color** by typing `/red` (or any available color). Type `/highlight` for a list of available colors.\
\
- Make a bullet into a **heading** by typing `/h1`, `/h2`, or `/h3`, or pressing Ctrl+Alt+1/2/3 (Cmd+Opt+1 on a Mac).\
\
\
# Do more with the /-menu and omnibar\
\
Press `/` while typing to bring up a menu where you can search through all formatting options and editor features.\
\
To edit multiple bullets at once, or perform actions that aren't connected to specific selected text, launch the [omnibar](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/7852647-using-the-omnibar) with Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac).\
