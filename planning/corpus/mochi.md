## Language learning

Study vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation using flashcards, built-in dictionaries, translation, and text-to-speech — all in one app. [Learn more](https://mochi.cards/)

### Built-in dictionaries

Instantly look up words in multiple languages without leaving your notes. [Learn more.](https://mochi.cards/docs/templates/dynamic-fields/)

### Text-to-speech

Hear your cards spoken aloud for better pronunciation and listening practice.


### Image search

Find the perfect image for any word or phrase to make your flashcards more visual and memorable.


### Auto-translate

Translate any text on your cards to or from your target language.


## Note-taking and studying

Take notes in Markdown, connect ideas, and turn your reading or research into long-term knowledge with Mochi's smart review system.


### References

Link notes both ways to build a connected web of ideas and discover new relationships.


### Tags

Categorize and group notes with simple, flexible tags for easier navigation and organization.


### Filters and views

Instantly find what you need with customizable filters, saved views, and powerful search options.


### AI generated text

Create example sentences or study prompts using the latest AI models.
