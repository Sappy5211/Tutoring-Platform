Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Cuemath program?

Cuemath is a live, one-on-one online math tutoring program for students from Kindergarten to Grade 12. Every session is conducted on our proprietary interactive learning platform, led by a real expert tutor. Not a recorded video, not an AI, not a group class, and not just a zoom call. Just your child and their dedicated tutor, working together 2-3 times a week on a learning plan built specifically around their needs, their pace, and their school curriculum.

### Is Cuemath Worth It?

Absolutely, if you want your child to truly understand math, not just memorize it. Cuemath is built for families who want a real expert tutor working one-on-one with their child in every session. Not automated worksheets. Not an AI-only platform. Our tutors don't just hand out worksheets to solve. Through our interactive learning platform, they guide your child to discover answers on their own, building a deep conceptual understanding for lasting confidence. If you want your child to know the why behind every solution — not just the how — Cuemath is worth it. See for yourself with a free trial class.

### Are Cuemath classes only online?

Yes. All Cuemath classes are conducted online, on our proprietary platform Cuemath Leap, and that's a deliberate choice, not a limitation. There are no offline centers in the U.S. Being online means your child learns from the world's best tutors, not just the best tutor available in your neighborhood. And because every session is one-on-one on Cuemath Leap, with a collaborative digital whiteboard, interactive tools, and real-time feedback, your child gets more focused attention in a single Cuemath class than most children get in a week of school.

### Is Cuemath a gamified platform?

Yes, but not in the way most people mean it. Many platforms use gamification as a substitute for real teaching — points, badges, and leaderboards to keep children clicking. Cuemath is different. Our platform, Cuemath LEAP, uses interactive tools, visual simulations, and engaging problem-solving activities — all to support deep conceptual understanding, and is guided by an expert tutor. The goal is never to keep your child entertained. It's to develop deep mathematical thinking.

### Who are Cuemath tutors? Are they real, qualified teachers?

Yes, every Cuemath session is led by a real, live human tutor. Not an AI, not a bot, not a recorded lesson. But not just any teacher. Only the top 1% of tutor applicants make it through our selection process. Every tutor is evaluated for subject expertise, teaching ability, and the ability to work with children — and then trained specifically in the Cuemath methodology before they ever teach a single class. Your child gets the same tutor every session, someone who gets to know how they think, where they struggle, and what motivates them. That consistency is what builds real progress.

### What language are Cuemath classes taught in? Are tutors fluent in English?

All Cuemath classes are conducted in English. Our tutors are based primarily in India with degrees in Mathematics, Engineering, or Education, and strong communication skills are a non-negotiable part of how we select them. Every tutor is evaluated not just on subject expertise, but on their ability to explain concepts clearly, adapt to different learning styles, and make students feel comfortable enough to ask questions freely. Families across the US, UK, Australia, and 80+ countries learn with Cuemath every week, and communication consistently ranks among the most praised aspects in parent reviews.

### What is the Cuemath Leap Platform?

Cuemath Leap is our proprietary online classroom, built specifically for one-on-one math tutoring, from the ground up. Unlike a generic video call, Cuemath Leap is designed around how children actually learn math. It features MathCanvas, a collaborative digital whiteboard where your child and their tutor work through problems together in real time. It includes interactive simulations, visual tools, & curriculum-aligned practice that adapts to your child's level and pace. Every session on Cuemath Leap is live, private, child-safe, and ad-free. No distractions. No third-party tools. Just your child and their tutor, in an environment built for one purpose: helping your child truly understand math.

### Does Cuemath have an App?

Yes. The Cuemath app is free to download for everyone — enrolled or not. For students, the highlight is **Math Gym**, a daily 15-minute brain training workout with math games, puzzles, and logic challenges that build fluency, speed, understanding, accuracy, and problem-solving skills. Just like physical fitness, mathematical fitness needs daily training. For parents, the app provides real-time progress tracking, performance reports, tutor communication, and class rescheduling — all in one place. [Download the Cuemath app](https://cuemath.onelink.me/Zb5o/49lj00od)

### My child has specific learning requirements. Is your program flexible enough?

Yes. Every learning plan is built around your child's unique needs, be it remedial support, homework help, exam prep, or enrichment beyond school. If your child has specific learning differences (dyslexia, dyscalculia, ADHD), please contact your admissions counselor so we can match them with the best-suited tutor.

### Can your tutors teach the topics covered in my child's school or curriculum?

Absolutely. Our program is well-designed, with curricula in the US, UK, India, Australia, New Zealand, and 80+ countries — including Common Core, Cambridge IGCSE, IB, CBSE, ICSE, and more.
