#### Real Time Insights, Real Results

I particularly value the real-time feedback available for teachers, it makes tracking student progress seamless and allows for targeted support where it's needed most. Setting up a course that follows our Maths Scheme of Work was straightforward, which means teachers can easily set appropriate work that aligns perfectly with our curriculum. This also gives students a clear picture of where they are in the course and provides them with an excellent platform for independent study, practice, and revision ahead of assessments.

* * *

Teacher - Cranleigh School

#### Effortless Centralised Homework

My school makes very effective use of the DFM website right from primary level up to A-Level Maths and Further Maths. We use it to set a weekly centralised homework task to every child in Key Stage 3 - it is incredibly easy and efficient to use as the pupils receive instant feedback and teachers need not do a ton of marking after the event, as used to be the case with traditional homework. It has a unique feature which allows each pupil to be set a different collection of questions, which saves them from the temptation to share answers.
